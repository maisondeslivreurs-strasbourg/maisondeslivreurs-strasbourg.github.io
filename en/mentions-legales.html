Personal data

What this website does not do

This website sets no cookies, does not measure visitor numbers and has no forms. The fonts are hosted with the website: no data is sent to Google or any other third party when you visit.

When you contact us

If you contact us by email, in the WhatsApp group or on Instagram, we use your contact details and message only to reply to you and, if you ask, to send you the association's news. This information is never sold or passed on to third parties. It is deleted at the latest three years after our last exchange.

In the WhatsApp group, your phone number and profile name are visible to the other members of the group.

WhatsApp and Instagram are Meta services: their own privacy rules apply when you use them.

The host

Like any host, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete information about you, or object to its use, by writing to lamaisondeslivreurs.strasbourg@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied, you can file a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority).

Accessibility

We want this website to be readable by everyone, including people who read little French or use a screen reader:

  • the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, designed for people with low vision;
  • strong colour contrast and large text;
  • keyboard navigation, and large links and buttons on phones;
  • animations switched off if your device is set to reduce motion.

This website has not yet been formally audited for accessibility. If you run into any difficulty, write to us: we will fix the problem or send you the information in another format.
