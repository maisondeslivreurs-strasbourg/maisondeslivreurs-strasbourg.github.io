The project

A resource centre for app-based delivery riders, built with them.

A Strasbourg association registered in September 2026, supported by the Labo des partenariats and part of the national network of Maisons des Livreurs. The space is set to open in March 2027.

The issue

They deliver our meals in all weathers, seven days a week. Between two orders, they have nowhere to shelter, rest or be heard. So we are building with them a house that belongs to them.

What the SANTÉ-COURSE study shows

63 h of work per week on average
58,7 % have already had a work accident
32 % have no health cover at all
42,5 % went at least one whole day without a meal in the past year

Source: SANTÉ-COURSE report, Médecins du Monde · IRD · INED (2026), a survey of more than 1,000 riders in Paris and Bordeaux.

And in Strasbourg?

  • Several thousand rider accounts are active in the Strasbourg metropolitan area, yet there is no space dedicated to them.
  • Many of them live in the city's priority neighbourhoods: Neuhof, Meinau, Koenigshoffen, Rotterdam, Citadelle…
  • Some riders are deactivated by the apps even though they hold a valid receipt for their residence permit renewal: overnight, they lose all income.

The key study

The SANTÉ-COURSE survey interviewed 1,004 riders face to face, in five languages. The Paris and Bordeaux Maisons des Livreurs were partners. Nearly six in ten riders report experiencing discrimination at work.

Our response

One place, six missions, specialist partners.

Open to all riders, whatever the app, free and unconditional. Everyone finds shelter, answers and a place to take action.

01

Welcome

A space to rest and meet: coffee, toilets, phone and battery charging, with opening hours that fit delivery shifts.

02

Access your rights

Paperwork, account deactivations, digital documents, with Reconnect and its digital safe.

03

Look after your health

Health drop-ins and interpreting with Migrations Santé Alsace, which also trains the team and volunteers.

04

Speak French, find what's next

French practice and guidance towards jobs and training with Tunaweza.

05

Repair and ride safely

A self-repair workshop suited to e-bikes, plus road safety.

06

Raise awareness and speak out

A charter for restaurants, film debates, field data and advocacy with the national network.

The funding plan

Foundations

The Fondation de France has been asked for €50,000 a year over three years. Other corporate foundations are being approached.

Local authorities and the State

Contrat de ville 2027 (State and Eurométropole), City and Eurométropole under the social and solidarity economy, European Collectivity of Alsace, FDVA.

Own income

Memberships, a bike workshop with solidarity pricing open to all, then training: a share that grows every year.

In-kind contributions

Volunteering, loaned rooms, donated equipment: €100,000 of contributions valued over three years, on top of the budget.

Supported by the Labo des partenariats

The Labo des partenariats supports us in structuring the project, finding a space and bringing partners and funders on board.

Our rule: any funding from a delivery app or a company is governed by an ethics charter, with no branding in return and no say over our work.

Governance and impact

Governed with riders, evaluated with our partners.

Governance

An association under Alsace-Moselle local law, registered with the Strasbourg court. A collective board and two voting groups: riders and other members. Partner organisations sit in an advisory group.

Supported by the Labo des partenariats (Start-Up de Territoire programme), working with CoopCycle-Association and the national network of Maisons des Livreurs.

What we measure

  • riders welcomed and supported, including the share living in priority neighbourhoods;
  • successful rights procedures, referrals to care, jobs or training;
  • repairs carried out at the workshop;
  • riders involved in decisions and in running the space;
  • volunteers trained and hours given;
  • restaurants signing the charter and people reached by awareness work.

A joint review every year with riders, partners and funders. The figures also feed into the network's advocacy.

A model that works

Paris, Bordeaux, Nantes… and now Strasbourg.

Strasbourg is joining the national network of Maisons des Livreurs, alongside CoopCycle-Association.

Paris

Maison des coursiers · 2021

The first space of its kind. A year after it opened, 22 people it supported had found a job, 10 of them on permanent contracts.
