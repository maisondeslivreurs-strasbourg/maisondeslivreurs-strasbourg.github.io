What is La Maison des Livreurs?

A place to take a break, and to take back control.

Walk in without paperwork or an appointment, whatever the app. You find answers, and you learn to stand up for your rights yourself.

Take a break

Warm and dry between orders: coffee, toilets, microwave, wifi, battery charging.

Stand up for your rights

Residence permits, account deactivation, taxes, self-employed status: we support you so you understand your rights and handle the paperwork yourself.

Take charge of your health

Drop-in sessions with Migrations Santé Alsace to be listened to, understand what you need and choose your care.

French & digital skills

Conversation partners with Tunaweza, a digital safe for your papers with Reconnect.

Bike workshop

Learn to fix your bike yourself and know the e-bike rules so you can ride without fines.

Meet up, get organised

Riders' Cafés, group sessions, a link with the unions: to make our voices heard together. And for what comes next: jobs, training, housing.

Our pledge to riders

What the house promises you, every time you walk through the door.

  1. 01

    Always free

    Welcome, coffee and support cost nothing.

  2. 02

    No conditions

    No questions about your papers, your story or the account you use.

  3. 03

    Independent

    A non-profit: not the city council, not the prefecture, not the police, not an app.

  4. 04

    Confidential

    No name, number, photo or story is shared without your consent.

  5. 05

    Honest

    No empty promises: we explain your options, connect you with the right partners and stay by your side.

  6. 06

    With you, not for you

    You decide what you need, at your own pace. You can say no, go off on a delivery and come back.

A shared house

This place is yours. Bring it to life.

La Maison des Livreurs is not a service you just use: it is an association that riders build and run. It works collectively: decisions are made together.

01

Deciding together

Riders have been part of the project from the start. Opening hours, activities, house rules: the house is organised with the people who use it.

02

Become a member

You can join the association, come to the general meetings and put forward your ideas. Your voice counts as much as anyone else's.

03

Passing it on

Translating, welcoming a newcomer, fixing a bike, running a Café: everyone has something to share with others.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

Is it really free?+

Yes. Welcome, coffee and support are free, with no membership required.

Do I need papers to come?+

No. You won't be asked for any document to come in, take a break or ask a question.

I don't speak much French.+

No problem. Our volunteers speak several languages, and this website is available in French, English, Arabic and Pashto.

Can I get involved in the association?+

Yes, that's the whole point. You can become a member, come to the general meetings, suggest an activity or run a Café. The house is built with the people who bring it to life.

Does it matter which app I deliver for?+

No. Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Stuart, a co-op or anything else: the house is open to all riders.
